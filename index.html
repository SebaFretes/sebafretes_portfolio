Control de Gastos

This app allows users to easily keep track of their expenses. Users can enter their total budget at the start and record each expense with details such as the name of the product or service, the amount, the date of the expense, and the corresponding category. Expenses are displayed immediately and can be edited or deleted, as well as filtered by category for better visualization. There is also an option to reset the application and start with a new budget at any time.
